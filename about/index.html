Academic Biography

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Management at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, where I lead the DRIVE Lab (Decision-making, Robotics and Intelligent Vehicles). My research focuses on intelligent decision-making systems, combining automated planning, reinforcement learning, and optimal control, with applications in robotics and transportation.

Before joining Ben-Gurion University, I was an AI Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Toronto and a Faculty Affiliate Researcher at the Vector Institute. I earned my PhD at the Technion, where my doctoral research addressed integrated planning and control for hybrid domains. My background spans both academic research and industry, including research internships at VMware Research and the Bosch Center for Artificial Intelligence, and earlier engineering and technical leadership roles.
